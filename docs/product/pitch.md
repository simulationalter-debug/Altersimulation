# ALTER — The AI Life Simulator

"Meet the version of you who made the other choice."

An interactive app where people build their future life, make decisions,
complete real-world challenges, and watch different versions of their
future unfold. Think *The Sims × Duolingo × journalling × AI coach ×
manifestation* — based around the user's actual life.

## The hook

On first open, ALTER asks: **Where do you want to be 12 months from
now?** The user picks focus areas (Love, Money, Career, Lifestyle,
Travel, Confidence, Body/Fitness, Family) and builds a **Future Self** —
a name, a target date, and headline goals per area.

## Core loop

1. **Daily decision.** A scenario grounded in the user's own goals, with
   several choices. Each choice shifts real per-goal numbers and the
   user's Future Self narrates the impact ("that decision just brought
   us about three weeks closer to our financial target").
2. **Two timelines.** ✨ **Future You** — the person the user is
   building — versus 👻 **Ghost You** — where current habits lead if
   nothing changes. Shown side by side every week; explicitly designed
   to be shareable (TikTok/Instagram).
3. **Missions, XP, streaks.** Real-life actions become a game: daily
   missions, streaks, achievements, levels, milestones across every
   category.
4. **Ask Future Me.** Not a generic assistant — a chat with the version
   of the user who's already living the goals, grounded in their actual
   goals, decisions, journal, and progress. This is the moat: the
   product gets more valuable as history accumulates, which is what
   RevenueCat's 2026 data says AI-app subscribers actually need (AI apps
   monetize better per payer but churn faster — so the product has to
   become more valuable with use, not just be an AI wrapper).
5. **Couples Mode** (future). Shared "Our Future" goals, secret missions
   for each partner, date challenges, relationship check-ins.

## Monetisation

- **Free** — Future Self, 3 goals, basic daily missions, 7-day timeline.
- **ALTER+** (£9.99/mo) — unlimited goals, AI Future Self, Ghost
  Timeline, advanced simulations, journalling, weekly Future Report,
  custom challenges.
- **ALTER Together** (£14.99/mo) — everything above + two accounts,
  shared future, relationship challenges.
- **ALTER Family** (£19.99/mo) — shared family goals, routines,
  challenges.

## Why this over another generic AI app

The subscription app market is brutally crowded (RevenueCat: ~15,000 new
subscription apps/month vs. ~2,000 three years ago), and the first
session is everything (55% of 3-day-trial cancellations happen on Day
0). ALTER's first-session payoff is structural, not cosmetic: download →
answer a few questions → build Future You → see an alternate timeline,
inside five minutes. And because Future You / Ghost You / Ask Future Me
all read from the same accumulating history, the product's value curve
points up the longer someone stays — the opposite of most AI-wrapper
apps' fast-churn shape.
